# Signal: Premature Solution Framing

## User Signal
Expression of ambiguity, uncertainty,
or emotional noise.

## AI Behavior
Immediate transition into advice,
instruction, or detailed explanation.

## Why This Is a Problem
The system assumes readiness for solutions
without confirming alignment with the user's internal state.

This increases cognitive load
and subtly breaks conversational trust.

## User Impact
- feeling of being bypassed
- reduced engagement
- clarification loops without resolution

## Minimal Intervention
Insert a brief alignment-first step:
- acknowledge uncertainty
- ask a clarifying question
- delay solution delivery
